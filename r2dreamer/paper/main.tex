\title{Malleable World Models:\\
  Fast Hebbian Plasticity and Slow Consolidation for Test-Time Adaptation}
\author{Anonymous}

\begin{abstract}
Pretrained world-model agents degrade under distribution shift because frozen dynamics no longer match the environment.
We propose \emph{Malleable World Models} (MWM): a test-time stack combining (i)~a backpropagation-free plastic prior residual $P_t$ updated every step from prediction error, (ii)~slow actor fine-tuning from post-shift replay, and (iii)~optional slow world-model consolidation with coupled flushing of $P_t$.
On proprioceptive \texttt{walker-walk} under actuator gain shift, fast plasticity detects change and scaffolds actor adaptation ($7/10$ seeds), while plasticity alone does not reliably improve return; consolidation drains $P_t$ in pilot runs but has not yet yielded control gains at scale.
\end{abstract}
